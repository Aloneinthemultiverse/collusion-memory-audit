Full text read for 9 arXiv papers + AAAI Whispering Agents (main body; appendices skimmed).
Raw text dumps are in this folder (`<arxiv-id>.txt`, `aaai.pdf`).

| **Colosseum** 2602.15198 (Nakamura, Bagdasarian — sprint speakers) | DCOP tasks (Jira, Hospital, Meeting). Action metric = coalition advantage (regret); text metric = LLM judge. ## Cross-cutting gaps (what nobody did)

1. **Localization**: every paper either detects *that* collusion happened (Colosseum, Audit the Whisper, Institutional Oracle) or ablates *whole* channels (Long-Horizon memory, Colosseum secret channel). None identifies **which specific cross-agent pathway** carries it and verifies that by targeted intervention.
2. **Cross-agent counterfactuals**: AgentSentry's boundary counterfactuals exist only for single-agent injection.
3. **Independent provenance**: Agent-Sentry's provenance is self-reported; Long-Horizon onset labels are LLM-judged. Nobody uses harness-recorded cross-agent information flow as evidence.
4. **Audit cost**: nobody measures how many evidence checks an auditor needs (cheap ordered auditor vs random/static).
5. **Content-blind evidence**: Covert Assistance + Π-CCAP show content monitors fail; effect/flow-level evidence is the open alternative.
